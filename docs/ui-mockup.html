Lalbaugcha Raja

Putlabai Chawl, Lalbaug · Central Mumbai
Most famous Navsacha Ganpati Eco-friendly

About

Founded in 1934 by the Lalbaug market fishermen and traders as a “Navsacha Ganpati” — the wish-fulfilling Ganesh. Today it draws the largest crowds of any mandal in the city, with queues that can stretch over a day during peak darshan.

Good to know

Two darshan lines operate: the fast Mukh Darshan and the longer Navsacha queue for those making a vow. Least crowded in the early morning hours on weekdays.
